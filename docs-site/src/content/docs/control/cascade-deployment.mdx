This guide is for integrators who put [`Cascade`](/control/cascade/)
between a state estimator and the [mixer](/control/mixer/). It covers what
to set, what the cascade needs from the rest of the stack, and how to wire
it. The [developer guide](/control/cascade/) has the loops and the API.

## Parameters

`CascadeConfig<T>` is a plain aggregate of the three loops' configs and the
two dividers. `cascade_config_from(vehicle)` derives all of it from the
vehicle's `QuadrotorParams`, and `cascade_config_valid()` (I1) must hold
before a cascade is built. For RP-1 (1 kg, J = (0.003, 0.003, 0.005) kg·m²,
motor lag τ = 20 ms, 12 N per rotor):

| Field | RP-1 | Unit | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rate.inertia` | 0.003, 0.003, 0.005 | kg·m² | the vehicle's J |
| `rate.axis[i].kp` | 50 | 1/s | 1/τ |
| `rate.axis[i].kd` | 0.4 | 1 | 2·0.7 − 1 |
| `rate.axis[i].ki` | 250 | 1/s² | k_p·ω_n/10 |
| `rate.axis[i].derivative_tau` | 2 | ms | τ/10 |
| `rate.axis[i].output_max` | 622, 622, 76.8 | rad/s² | the full differential thrust over J |
| `rate.axis[i].integral_max` | 156, 156, 19.2 | rad/s² | a quarter of the output limit |
| `attitude.gain` | 10, 10, 5 | 1/s | ω_n/5, and ω_n/10 for yaw |
| `attitude.max_rate` | 10, 10, 4 | rad/s | fixed |
| `position.position_gain` | 1.56 | 1/s | ω_n/32 |
| `position.velocity[i].kp`, `.ki` | 3.57, 1.28 | 1/s, 1/s² | ω_n/14, k_v²/10 |
| `position.velocity[i].output_max`, `.integral_max` | 9.81, 4.90 | m/s² | g, g/2 |
| `position.max_speed_xy`, `_up`, `_down` | 8, 3, 2 | m/s | fixed |
| `position.max_tilt` | 0.7 (40°) | rad | fixed |
| `position.min_thrust`, `max_thrust` | 0.98, 38.4 | N | 10 % of hover; 80 % of 4·T_max |
| `attitude_divider`, `position_divider` | 4, 20 | ticks | 250 Hz and 50 Hz at a 1 kHz rate loop |

Every row is a derived default; set a field directly to override it. The
motor lag and the inertia matter most: the rate loop's gains come from τ,
and its torque from J. Measure both on your vehicle (τ from a step on a
thrust stand, J from a swing test or CAD) rather than keep RP-1's.

## Loop rates

The defaults assume a 1 kHz rate loop: call `update()` once per tick with dt
= 1 ms. Another rate-loop period works as long as dt is what you pass: the
PIDs integrate over dt, and the position loop over `dt · position_divider`.
Keep the outer loops at about 250 Hz and 50 Hz when you change it, by
changing the dividers: the gains assume each loop is sampled well above its
bandwidth. `position_divider` must be a multiple of `attitude_divider`.

## The state the cascade needs

`VehicleState` is position and velocity in the world frame (z up, m and
m/s), the attitude as a body-to-world unit quaternion, and the body rates in
rad/s. It comes from your estimator. The cascade does no filtering of its
own, beyond the rate loop's derivative filter, and the closed-loop tests
feed it the true state. So:

- **Body rates** should be the gyro, filtered for the motors' noise (see the
  [RPM notch](/dsp/filters/)), with as little delay as you can manage. The
  rate loop is the fastest loop, and delay there costs phase margin first.
- **Delay.** The tests show the loops stay stable, with at most 25 %
  overshoot, with 4 ms of command delay combined with slower motors and more
  inertia. Treat 4 ms end to end as the budget, and measure your stack's.
- **Frames.** A state in another convention (NED, FRD) must be converted
  before it reaches the cascade. A wrong sign on one axis turns its loop
  into positive feedback.

## Modes, arming, and reset

- **Position mode** (`update(x, PositionSetpoint, dt)`) for autonomous
  flight. Give the setpoint the path's velocity and acceleration as
  feed-forward when you have them: without it the vehicle trails the
  setpoint (2.7 m on the explainer's square at 5 m/s).
- **Attitude mode** (`update(x, AttitudeTarget, dt)`) for a pilot's angle
  mode, or for an outer loop of your own. You supply the collective thrust.
- **Switching** from attitude to position mode resets the position loop, so
  the vehicle flies toward the position setpoint from where it is, with
  fresh integrators. Set the setpoint to the current position at the switch
  if you want it to hold.
- **Arming.** Call `reset()` when the vehicle arms. On the ground the loops'
  integrators wind up against the ground's reaction, and the mixer spins the
  rotors up whenever torque is asked for (airmode). Hold the cascade reset,
  and the motors at idle, until take-off; see the mixer's
  [arming and idle](/control/mixer-deployment/#arming-idle-and-the-ground).

## Wiring it into a loop

Once per rate tick, in this order:

```cpp
const control::Wrench<double> w = cascade.update(state, setpoint, dt);
const control::MixerOutput<double> out = mixer.mix(w);
cascade.feedback(out);  // the integrators see this tick's cuts next tick
const model::RotorCommand<double> u = mixer.to_rotor_command(out.thrusts);
esc.write(u);
```

Call `feedback()` with the result of mixing the wrench `update()` just
returned, before the next `update()`. Without it the rate integrators wind
up while the mixer cuts, and the vehicle overshoots once the cut ends.

## Limits and what they cut

| Limit | RP-1 | When it cuts | What holds the integrators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal speed setpoint | 8 m/s | a far position setpoint | (a velocity setpoint, not integrated) |
| Climb and descent speed | 3, 2 m/s | a far vertical setpoint | (as above) |
| Collective thrust | 0.98 to 38.4 N | a hard climb or descent | the cut, on the next position tick |
| Tilt | 40° | a hard horizontal acceleration | the cut, on the next position tick |
| Velocity-loop acceleration | ±g per axis | a large velocity error | the PID's own output limit |
| Body-rate setpoint | 10, 10, 4 rad/s | a large attitude error | (a rate setpoint, not integrated) |
| Angular acceleration | 622, 622, 76.8 rad/s² | a large rate error | the PID's own output limit |
| Rotor thrust, in the mixer | 0 to 12 N | any of the above at the edges | the mixer's report, through `feedback()` |

The collective limit leaves 20 % of the rotors' thrust free for torque, so
in position mode the mixer keeps room for the tilt even at the top of the
collective. In attitude mode
your collective goes straight to the mixer, so a full-throttle command
leaves the mixer to give up yaw, and then the collective, to keep the tilt.

## Retuning

Change the gains when the vehicle differs from what `cascade_config_from()`
assumes: a different motor lag, a different airframe stiffness, or a
measured delay larger than 4 ms. Keep the ratios between the loops: each
loop at most about a fifth as fast as the one inside it. The
[roll-step widget](/control/cascade-explainer/#2-the-inner-loops-a-roll-step)
shows what happens when the attitude and rate gains come close. Check the
result with `cascade_config_valid()`, and fly the closed-loop tests in
`tests/sdk/cascade_closed_loop.cpp` against your vehicle's `QuadrotorParams`
before you fly.

A vehicle with a thrust-to-weight ratio of 1.25 or less cannot hover inside
the derived collective limit, and `cascade_config_from()` returns an invalid
config for it. Raise `position.max_thrust` only if you accept less torque
authority at the top of the throttle.

## Cost

About 16 ns per rate-loop tick on x86, for float and double alike
(`bench/cascade_bench`, GCC 15.2 Release, i7-12700K), averaged over the
schedule: a rate update costs about 8 ns, an attitude update 4 ns, and a
position update 40 ns. The operation count of each tick kind is bounded,
over generated states and setpoints (`tests/sdk/cascade_resource.cpp`): a
rate-only tick runs 72 arithmetic operations, an attitude tick at most 122
and one square root, and a position tick at most 302 and ten calls (square
roots, sine, and cosine). Measure on the flight processor before budgeting.

## Scalar types

The unit tests and three of the closed-loop tests (hover hold, the 30° roll
step, and the 1 m steps) run in double and float. The cascade uses
`+ − × ÷`, comparisons, `sqrt`, `sin`, `cos`, and `isfinite`.

## Consumer contract

| | |
|---|---|
| Preconditions | `cascade_config_valid(cfg)` at construction; a finite state with a unit attitude; a finite setpoint; dt > 0, else the last wrench is returned unchanged |
| Postconditions | A wrench whose thrust is the target's; rate setpoints within their limits; in position mode, the target's thrust within the collective limits and its tilt within the tilt limit |
| Faults | None reported: a precondition violation is an `assert` in Debug builds, and dt ≤ 0 holds the output |
| State | Each loop's PID integrators and held outputs, the tick counter, the mode, and the stored mixer report; `reset()` clears them |
| Resources | No allocation, no exceptions, no system calls; a bounded operation count per tick kind |
| Not provided | Sensor filtering or estimation, arming and idle, throttle-PID attenuation, I-term relax, gain scheduling, INDI, drag compensation, setpoint differentiation, staleness handling of an external rate command |
