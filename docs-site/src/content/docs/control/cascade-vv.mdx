This plan takes [`Cascade`](/control/cascade/) to grade **A** under the
[building block standard](/standards/building-blocks/). It lists where the
cascade stands against each requirement, the animations that illustrate its
checks, the evidence ledger, and a sequence of PRs.

**Scope.** The cascade is a controller defined in discrete time: four loops
on a fixed schedule, with PIDs, limits, and an attitude law. There is no
continuous model to converge to, so verification checks that each loop
computes its law exactly, that the limits and invariants hold for any
input, and that the loops together fly the closed-loop criteria stated
before the tests ran. That closed-loop evidence rests on the
[quadrotor model](/model/quadrotor/) as the plant, which has no independent
reference yet. It is design verification on a nominal plant. Validation
needs flight data (L1–L4, I3).

**Consumers.** racing-drone's B0 baseline: `update()` once per 1 kHz rate
tick in position and attitude mode, its wrench to `mix()`, and the mixer's
report back through `feedback()`.

### The closed-loop criteria

| Criterion (nominal plant) | Pass if |
|---|---|
| Hover hold, 10 s, from a disturbed start | Error < 1 cm after 2 s |
| 30° roll step | 90 % in ≤ 0.25 s, overshoot ≤ 10 %, within 2° by 0.6 s |
| 180° roll step | Within 2° by 1.5 s; −q flies identically |
| Tilt and yaw step | The tilt converges as fast as with no yaw step |
| 1 m step in x and in z | Overshoot ≤ 10 %, within 2 % by 3 s |
| Waypoint square, 10 m sides, commanded peak 5 m/s | Cross-track RMS ≤ 0.3 m, max ≤ 0.6 m; the flown top speed within 10 % of the commanded peak |
| 2 s of thrust saturation | Overshoot within 5 points of an unsaturated step |

### The schedule (BB-T1)

`update()` promises that the attitude loop runs on every 4th tick and the
position loop on every 20th, each holding its output in between. The unit
test `cascade: outer loops run on their dividers and hold in between`
checks it on a fixed state. The scenario checks it in closed loop, at every
tick.

### Anti-windup in closed loop (BB-I2)

Each velocity PID freezes its integral while the position loop cut its axis
on the last tick. The criterion: a climb that holds the thrust limit for
more than 2 s overshoots no more than an unsaturated step.

## Milestones

| # | PR scope | Requirements | Grade after |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | The envelope in `cascade.hpp`: thrust-to-weight, the delay budget, the state it assumes, the loop rates; `noexcept` on `update()`; every closed-loop test in float; posit and cfloat runs and the minimum precision; sanitizer preset | S2, S4, S5, T3 | — |
| M2 | Metamorphic tests: a mirror in y swaps the roll and yaw signs, a rotation of the world about z rotates the flight; property tests: generated vehicles inside the envelope, flown with their derived configs, meet a stability and overshoot bound; golden traces in `tests/` | V4, T4, R4 | **B** |
| M3 | Fuzz target for `update()` (any finite input keeps the invariants and a finite wrench); coverage report; comparison with PX4's multicopter rate, attitude, and position controllers on shared setpoints | T5, T6, V6 | B |
| M4 | Datasheet: each loop's step and frequency response, margins against τ, J, and delay, and the cost per type and compiler; scenario runner and monitor | C1–C4, D1–D3 | B |
| M5 | The cascade in the `sim/` scenario suite: sensor noise and delay from an estimator, actuator quantization, gusts, rotor loss, sensor dropout; lockstep and real-time timing | BB-I1, BB-I2, BB-I4 | **A** |
| M6 | RP-1 flight data: the roll step and the waypoint square flown and simulated with the same gains; processor in the loop on the flight MCU; racing-drone contract tests | L1–L4, BB-I3, BB-I5, R3 | A+ |

M2 is the step that matters most: the metamorphic and property tests check
the cascade against relations it must satisfy for any vehicle, not only
against RP-1's numbers. M5 shares the `sim/` harness with the
[PID's M6](/control/pid-vv/#milestones), the
[mixer's M6](/control/mixer-vv/#milestones), and the
[quadrotor model's M9](/model/quadrotor-vv/#milestones).
