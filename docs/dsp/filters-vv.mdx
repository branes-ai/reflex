This plan takes [the gyro filters](filters.md), `Biquad` and `RpmNotch`, to
grade **A** under the [building block standard](../standards/building-blocks.md).
It lists:
- where the filters stand against each requirement;
- the acceptance criteria;
- the animations that illustrate the checks;
- the evidence ledger;
- a sequence of PRs.

**Scope.** A filter's correctness is a property of its transfer function,
so most of the verification is against analytic answers and needs no
physical reference: the designs, the measured gains, the group delay, and
an independent implementation. The open question is physical: whether a
vehicle's vibration actually sits at the rotor harmonics, with amplitudes
the notches can take out. Spectral tests on RP-1 flight logs answer it
(the [control guide's](../control/quadrotor-control.md) F3), and they are
validation (L1–L4).

**Consumers.** racing-drone's B0 baseline: the gyro through `RpmNotch` and a
low-pass `Biquad` per axis, at the gyro rate, ahead of the
[cascade's](../control/cascade.mdx) rate loop. The consumer composes the
chain and chooses its settings (racing-drone#16).

### Acceptance criteria

Each tolerance is derived from a bound, never tuned to pass:
- for the start-up transient, the impulse response's tail;
- for rounding, a per-step bound carried through 1/A(z).

| Criterion | Pass if |
|---|---|
| Butterworth and first-order designs | DC gain 1 and $1/\sqrt{2}$ at $f_c$ to 1e-12; Butterworth magnitude monotone |
| Measured tone gains, 20 frequencies | Match `response()` within the derived bound |
| Notch | Analytic gain at $f_0$ ≤ 1e-12; a tone at $f_0$ attenuated ≥ 60 dB (double), ≥ 40 dB (float); −3 dB edges where the closed form puts them |
| Group delay | Measured from tone phases, matches `group_delay()` to 50 µs |
| Delay budget | Rate-loop phase margin ≥ 45° on the nominal vehicle, with a Butterworth at 16× crossover and hover-speed RPM notches in the loop |
| RPM tracking | Four rotors spread ±5 %, ramped to 1.5× hover in 1 s: vibration residual ≤ −30 dB, 5 Hz motion within 2 % |
| Fast retuning | Full-range speed swings within one motor time constant keep the output within the series ℓ1 gain bound |
| Code-to-code | Designs and outputs match mp-dsp within the derived bounds |

The tests print what they measure. RPM tracking leaves −78.8 dB of the
vibration in double and float. With a 2 % error in the rotor speeds it
leaves −32 dB, inside the criterion. The phase margin is 53.0° with the
filters, against 60.1° without them.

mp-dsp is a test-only dependency. Its designs throw on invalid input and do
not build with `-fno-exceptions`
([mp-dsp#217](https://github.com/stillwater-sc/mp-dsp/issues/217)), so the
flight path keeps reflex's own.

### The notch's width (BB-V1)

The closed form puts the −3 dB edges of `notch(f0, q, fs)` at a width of
(f_s/π)·atan(sin ω₀ / 2q). The unit test measures steady-state tone gains at
the edges. The scenario measures the width from a sweep.

## Milestones

| # | PR scope | Requirements | Grade after |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Scalar types in `rpm_notch.hpp`, with the minimum precision (float) and why 16-bit types fail; sanitizer preset | S4, T3 | — |
| M2 | Metamorphic tests: superposition and scaling, a time shift that shifts the output, permuted axes that permute it; property tests: generated valid designs are stable with the specified gains, and generated speed sequences stay within the ℓ1 bound; golden traces in `tests/` | V4, T4, R4 | **B** |
| M3 | Fuzz targets for `update()`, `apply()`, and `update_rotor_speeds()`: any input keeps the invariants and a finite output; coverage report | T5, T6 | B |
| M4 | Datasheet: magnitude, phase, and group delay of each design, the delay budget against q and the low-pass cutoff, the cost per type and compiler; scenario runner and monitor | C1–C4, D1–D3 | B |
| M5 | The filters in the `sim/` scenario suite, between a vibrating, noisy gyro model and the cascade's rate loop; lockstep and real-time timing | BB-I1, BB-I2, BB-I4 | **A** |
| M6 | RP-1 flight logs: spectra that confirm the harmonics (F3), the residual measured on logged gyro data, the ESC speed error; processor in the loop; racing-drone contract tests | L1–L4, BB-I5, R3 | A+ |

M2 matters most for B: the metamorphic relations hold for any linear
filter, so they check the recurrence without reference values. M6 matters
most overall, because it is the only step that tests the assumption the
RPM notch rests on. M5 shares the `sim/` harness with the
[cascade's M5](../control/cascade-vv.mdx#milestones).
